Marco Artiano

Ph.D. student
Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz
Advisor: Hendrik Ranocha

I’m a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Mathematics of Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz, in the research group of Prof. Dr. Hendrik Ranocha. I am mainly interested in high-order structure-preserving methods, such as entropy-stable, entropy-conservative and well-balanced discretizations, as well as efficient time integration schemes, and their application to atmospheric flows.

Software contributions

    Atmospheric models built on Trixi.jl.

    Jacobian-free Newton–Krylov solvers and time integration methods.
